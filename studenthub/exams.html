Entrance Exams

Explore major entrance exams in India. Click an exam to view detailed information, eligibility, dates, syllabus, pattern and FAQs.

JEE Main

National-level entrance exam for admission to NITs, IIITs and CFTIs. Conducted by NTA in multiple sessions.

JEE Advanced

Gateway to IITs. Conducted annually for top performers of JEE Main.

NEET

Single medical entrance exam for MBBS, BDS and AYUSH courses.

TS EAMCET

State-level entrance exam for Engineering and Agriculture admissions.

BITSAT

University-level entrance test for admission to BITS Pilani campuses.

VITEEE

Entrance exam for admission to VIT B.Tech programmes.

AP EAMCET

Andhra Pradesh Engineering, Agriculture and Medical Common Entrance Test.

SRMJEEE

SRM Joint Engineering Entrance Examination for admission to SRM campuses.

GITAM

Engineering entrance exam for admission to GITAM University.
